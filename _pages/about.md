Hi! I am a Cognitive Science PhD candidate at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Meenakshi Khosla. I study how AI systems represent and understand meaning across vision and language.

My research examines what semantic structure different models share, how their internal representations relate to their behavior, and how we can evaluate fine-grained understanding. I approach these questions through representational analysis, computational method development, and controlled experiments informed by cognitive science. More broadly, I am interested in developing a scientific understanding of intelligence, whether natural or artificial.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Angela J. Yu on human value-based decision-making under uncertainty, combining Bayesian computational modeling and pupillometry to understand how people adapt their decisions in changing environments.
